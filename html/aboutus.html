About Us

Maru Charitable Hospital

Maru Charitable Hospital is dedicated to providing quality and affordable healthcare services to the community.

Our hospital has a team of experienced doctors, nurses and healthcare professionals who work together to provide the best possible treatment and care to our patients.

We believe that every patient deserves proper medical attention, compassionate care and a safe environment.

Our aim is to serve society by providing accessible, reliable and quality healthcare services to everyone.

History

Establishment Year:Maru Hospital Established in 1991,a dedicated non-profit and charitable healthcare center. Managed by the Shree Bidada Sarvodaya Trust, the hospital was founded to serve the dense working-class population and textile mill workers of Central Mumbai by offering low-cost, accessible medical, dental, and surgical treatments. Operating without corporate frills, it has maintained a functional, ward-based model that prioritizes affordability and direct community care.
Over the decades, Maru Hospital expanded its clinical capabilities beyond basic outpatient consultations to include full-fledged departments in orthopedics, ophthalmology, dental care, general surgery, and ENT. By integrating with the broader social and medical initiatives of the Bidada Sarvodaya Trust, the institution regularly coordinates medical camps and specialized procedures to aid underserved patients. Today, it remains a prominent, high-footfall medical landmark in Parel, providing 24/7 emergency services, diagnostics, and routine surgeries.
